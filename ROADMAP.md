# Roadmap

This is a living roadmap for **Incarna**. It is community-driven: items can be
proposed, discussed, and re-prioritized through
[GitHub Discussions](../../discussions) and [Issues](../../issues). Nothing here
is a promise of dates — it's a shared sense of direction.

## Vision

Embody conversational AI agents in **mixed reality**. Incarna aims to be a
simple link that anyone can plug into their own [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw)
setup and instantly meet their agents as talking 3D avatars standing in their
own room — driven by voice, on Meta Quest 3 or any WebXR browser, with zero
dependencies and no build step.

## Now / Near-term

- [ ] Streaming TTS (start speaking before the full reply is generated)
- [ ] Richer idle life — talking gestures, weight shifts, and natural glances
- [ ] Hand-tracking selection (interact and pick without controllers)
- [ ] More avatar and voice presets
- [ ] The 3D wall-mounted Board surface (html2canvas texture) as a follow-up to
      the current 2D Markdown Board

## Later

- [ ] Optional rendered 3D office environment
- [ ] A community action pack (`.vrma` animations)
- [ ] Charts in the Board
- [ ] Internationalization (i18n) and translations
- [ ] Accessibility improvements
