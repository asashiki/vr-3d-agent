# 90–120 Second Demo Script

## 0:00–0:15

Open the same HTTPS URL in desktop Chrome and Quest Browser. In Quest, click Enter MR and confirm the microphone permission once.

## 0:15–0:30

Mira appears in front of the viewer while the World Tray stays hidden. Point at Mira, hold Grip, move her to a clean spot in the room, and release. Tap Y if you want to reset her in front of you.

## 0:30–0:55

Hold A or X and say: “在我面前搭一个治愈系小花园，有一棵树、一张长椅、两盏灯和一些花。” The tray appears only when the first prop is placed. Keep the Agent Trace visible as the structured commands execute and at least five objects appear.

## 0:55–1:15

Hold A or X and say: “把左边的灯移到树旁边，树缩小一点，删掉右边的石头。” Point out that the bench and right lamp remain unchanged.

## 1:15–1:35

Grip/pinch the bench and move it. Then say “往前走一步” and show Mira take a short procedural step. Say: “保持长椅现在的位置，保存这个场景。” Refresh, then press Restore.

## 1:35–2:00

Tap B to hide the tray and leave Mira alone in the room. Show the Inspector, Scene Tool Reference, 30-item CC0 manifest and model license. Disconnect the live provider or switch to Replay and run the demo again to prove the fallback.
